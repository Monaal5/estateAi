PROPERTY & MARKET OVERVIEW
Harborview Commons · Austin, TX

Property Description

Harborview Commons is a 120-unit, garden-style apartment community built in 2004 across ten two- and three-story buildings on 6.8 acres. Amenities include a resort-style pool, 24-hour fitness center, dog park, clubhouse with co-working space, and 212 surface parking spaces plus 24 detached garages. In 2019 the seller replaced all roofs and repainted building exteriors. Units feature 9-foot ceilings, in-unit washer/dryer connections, and private patios or balconies.

Market Overview

The property is in the Riverside / East Riverside submarket, approximately 3.5 miles from downtown Austin and 6 miles from Austin-Bergstrom International Airport. Business Plan

The Sponsor will renovate 30 classic unit interiors (LVP flooring, quartz counters, stainless appliances, LED lighting) at approximately $11,000 per unit, funded from the $400,000 capital reserve along with common-area upgrades. The target rent premium is $125 per month per renovated unit.
